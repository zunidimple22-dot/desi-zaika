🍖 Mutton Nihari

Purani Dilli se Karachi tak ka shahi nashta! Raat bhar halki aanch par gala hua mutton, garha masaledar shorba — nihari lovers ki jannat.

Prep · 20 minCook · 3 hoursServes · 6Spice · Medium-Hot
Advertisement

Ingredients

  • 1 kg mutton shank (nalli wala gosht behtareen)
  • 1 cup oil or ghee
  • 3 tbsp nihari masala (homemade ya packet)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds (saunf) powder
  • 1/2 tsp dry ginger powder (sonth)
  • 4 tbsp wheat flour (atta) mixed in 1 cup water
  • Salt to taste
  • Garnish: ginger juliennes, green chilies, coriander, lemon wedges
  • 1 onion (sliced, for tarka)

Method

  1. Bari degchi mein oil garam karke pyaz halki sunahri karein. Ginger-garlic paste dal kar 2 minute bhunein.
  2. Mutton dal kar tez aanch par 10 minute bhunein jab tak gosht ka rang badal jaye aur pani khushk ho jaye.
  3. Nihari masala, haldi, lal mirch, saunf aur sonth powder dal kar achhi tarah mix karein aur 5 minute bhunein taake masale ki khushbu uth jaye.
  4. 6-7 cup garam pani dalen, ubal aane par aanch bilkul halki karke dhak dein. Ab sabar ka waqt hai — gosht ko 2.5 se 3 ghante halki aanch par galne dein. Beech mein ek-do baar check karein aur zaroorat ho to garam pani dalen.
  5. Jab gosht hadi se alag hone lage, to aate ka ghol (wheat flour + water) thoda thoda karke dalte jayein aur musalsal chamach chalayein taake gutliyan na banen. Shorba garha hone tak 10-15 minute pakayein.
  6. Namak check karein. Upar se adrak ke lachhe, hari mirchein, hara dhaniya aur lemon ke qatre dal kar kulcha naan ya khameeri roti ke saath serve karein.
Advertisement

Chef's Notes

  • Nalli (shank) wala gosht nihari ke liye best hai — hadi ka gooda shorbe ko lajawab banata hai.
  • Aanch hamesha halki rakhein; tez aanch par gosht sakht ho jata hai aur shorba patla rehta hai.
  • Nihari agli subah dobara garam karke khayein to taste aur gehra hota hai — isiliye deg bhar ke banayein!
  • Atta ka ghol dalte waqt chamach rukna nahi chahiye warna gutliyan ban jayengi.
