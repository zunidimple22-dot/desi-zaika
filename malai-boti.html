🍖 Chicken Malai Boti

Creamy, mild aur melt-in-mouth! Bachon aur halke masale pasand karne walon ke liye best BBQ — malai aur cheese ka naram jadoo.

Prep · 3 hours (marination)Cook · 20 minServes · 4Spice · Mild
Advertisement

Ingredients

  • 750 g boneless chicken (cubes)
  • 1/2 cup fresh cream (malai)
  • 1/4 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp cream cheese (optional, extra creamy)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp white pepper powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Butter for basting

Method

  1. Chicken cubes par lemon juice aur namak laga kar 15 minute rakh dein — is se boti naram hoti hai.
  2. Cream, dahi, cream cheese, tamam masale aur oil ko achhi tarah phent kar smooth marinade banayein. Chicken cubes is mein lipten aur 3-4 ghante fridge mein rakhein.
  3. Seekhon par cubes charhayein, darmiyan mein thoda faasla rakhein taake har taraf se sek aaye.
  4. Halke-garam koilon par rakhein — malai boti ko tez aanch nahi, darmiyani aanch chahiye warna cream jal jayegi. Har 3-4 minute mein palten aur makhan brush karein.
  5. 15-20 minute mein jab botiyan sunahri aur naram lagne lagen, utaar lein. Zyada der rakhne se sookh jayengi.
  6. Hari chutney aur naan ke saath serve karein — upar se thodi cream ki dhaar daalna na bhoolen!
Advertisement

Chef's Notes

  • White pepper hi use karein — kaali mirch se color kharab hota hai aur malai boti ka signature look chala jata hai.
  • Cream cheese na ho to 2 tbsp extra malai mila lein, farq nahi parega.
  • Malai boti ko hamesha taaza serve karein — thandi ho kar creamy texture khatam ho jata hai.
  • Bachon ke lunchbox ke liye behtareen: masala halka hai aur boti naram hai.
