🍗 Tandoori Chicken

Laal-surkh, chatpata aur dhuaan daar — tandoor ki mitti wali khushbu wala classic. Ghar ke oven mein bhi perfect ban sakta hai!

Prep · 6 hours (marination)Cook · 40 minServes · 4Spice · Hot
Advertisement

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (4 leg quarters, skin removed, deep cuts)
  • 1 cup hung curd
  • 2 tbsp tandoori masala
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp ajwain (carom seeds)
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 tbsp mustard oil
  • 1 tsp red food color (optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • Butter for basting
  • Mint chutney + onion rings

Method

  1. Chicken pieces par gehray cuts lagayein. Lemon juice, namak aur aadha tandoori masala laga kar 30 minute rakh dein (first marination).
  2. Hung curd mein bacha tandoori masala, tamam pise masale, ajwain, food color aur sarson ka tel mila kar garha paste banayein aur chicken par achhi tarah lagayein — cuts ke andar tak.
  3. 6 ghante ya raat bhar fridge mein marinate karein. Yeh step skip na karein — isi se masala hadi tak utarta hai.
  4. Oven ko 220°C par preheat karein. Chicken ko grill rack par rakhein (neeche tray zaroor rakhein taake drippings giren). 20 minute ek taraf pakayein.
  5. Palat kar makhan brush karein aur mazeed 15-20 minute pakayein jab tak upar charred spots aa jayein aur juices saaf niklen.
  6. Koilon ka dhuaan chahiye? Ek jalti hui koyli katori mein rakh kar chicken ke saath degchi mein 5 minute dhak dein — ghar mein tandoori dhuaan!
  7. Chaat masala aur lemon chhidak kar podine ki chutney ke saath serve karein.

Chef's Notes

  • Ajwain tandoori masale ka secret ingredient hai — iske baghair woh khaas khushbu nahi aati.
  • Chicken ko oven se nikaal kar 5 minute rest dein taake juices andar settle ho jayein — foran kaatne se sukha lagega.
  • Skin utarna na bhoolen warna masala andar nahi jayega aur skin jal jayegi.
  • Air fryer mein 200°C par 25 minute mein bhi zabardast banta hai — beech mein ek baar palatna zaroori hai.
