🧆 Chapli Kabab

Peshawar ka fakhar! Chapte, masaledar kabab — anar dana ki khatas, tamatar ka tukda upar, taway par khasta tala hua.

Prep · 30 minCook · 20 minServes · 5Spice · Hot
Advertisement

Ingredients

  • 750 g beef mince (coarse/mota keema)
  • 2 tomatoes (1 chopped, 1 sliced for topping)
  • 1 onion (finely chopped)
  • 2 tbsp anar dana (dried pomegranate seeds, crushed)
  • 2 tsp red chili flakes
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (crushed)
  • 1 tsp coriander seeds (crushed)
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 green chilies (chopped)
  • 1/2 cup coriander + mint (chopped)
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp gram flour (besan)
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil/ghee for shallow frying

Method

  1. Keeme ka pani achhi tarah nichor lein. Us mein kata pyaz, kate tamatar, anar dana, tamam masale, hari mirch, dhaniya-podina, anda aur besan dal kar achhi tarah mix karein.
  2. Mixture ko 20 minute fridge mein rakh dein taake set ho jaye aur tikkiyan banate waqt tooten nahi.
  3. Haathon ko geela karke moti chapti tikkiyan banayein (chapli kabab patla nahi hota). Har tikki ke upar tamatar ka gol qatra lagayein — yahi uski pehchan hai.
  4. Tawa ya flat pan mein darmiyani tel garam karke tikkiyan rakhein. Ek taraf 4-5 minute khasta hone tak sek lein, phir palten.
  5. Dusri taraf bhi sunahri-khasta hone tak talen. Tel kam lagayenge to kabab sookhe lagenge — chapli kabab ko tel chahiye!
  6. Naan, dahi ki chutney aur kahwa ke saath serve karein — Peshawar wali feeling ghar baithe!

Chef's Notes

  • Anar dana hi chapli kabab ki asli khatas hai — imli ya lemon se woh baat nahi aati.
  • Tikkiyan zyada patli na banayein warna toot jayengi; 1 inch motai ideal hai.
  • Keema mota (coarse) hona chahiye — bareek keeme se chapli kabab ka texture kharab hota hai.
  • Ek baar mein zyada tikkiyan na dalen, tawa bhar jayega to steam banegi aur khasta pan nahi aayega.
