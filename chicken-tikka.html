🔥 Chicken Tikka

Dahi-masalon mein doobi chicken tikka botiyan, koilon ki aanch par laal-surkh siki hui — har BBQ party ka superstar!

Prep · 4 hours (marination)Cook · 25 minServes · 4Spice · Hot
Advertisement

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken (tikka pieces: leg + chest, cuts lagayein)
  • 1 cup yogurt (pani nikala hua / hung curd)
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp chaat masala
  • 1/2 tsp orange-red food color (optional)
  • 3 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • Salt to taste
  • Butter for basting
  • Onion rings + mint chutney for serving

Method

  1. Chicken pieces par gehray cuts lagayein taake masala andar tak jaye. Lemon juice aur namak laga kar 15 minute rakh dein.
  2. Dahi ka pani nikaal kar (malmal ke kapre mein 30 minute latkayein) us mein tamam masale, adrak-lehsan paste, food color aur sarson ka tel mila kar garha paste banayein.
  3. Chicken ko is masale mein achhi tarah lipten — cuts ke andar bhi masala bharen. Kam az kam 4 ghante, behtar hai raat bhar fridge mein marinate karein.
  4. Seekhon par botiyan charhayein (ya grill rack par rakhein). Koile tez garam hon — tikka ko tez aanch chahiye taake bahar se seke aur andar juicy rahe.
  5. Har 4-5 minute mein palten aur pighla hua makhan brush karte jayein. Kul 20-25 minute mein tikka tayyar — jab masala khushk aur kinare halke jalay hue lagen.
  6. Chaat masala chhidak kar pyaz ke lachhon aur podine ki chutney ke saath serve karein.
Advertisement

Chef's Notes

  • Hung curd (pani nikla dahi) use karein — patla dahi masala ko behata hai aur tikka pheeka lagta hai.
  • Sarson ka tel tikka ko woh khaas dhaba-style taste deta hai jo normal oil se nahi aata.
  • Chicken ko marination se seedha thande koilon par na rakhein — koile achhi tarah garam hon taake tikka foran seke aur juices andar lock ho jayein.
  • Bachi hui tikka se agle din tikka sandwich ya tikka fried rice bana lein — kuch zaya nahi hoga!
